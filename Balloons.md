# Balloons

Panel **Bill of Materials** ‣ **Balloons**: Front, Side, Top or Isometric view. One numbered balloon (5 mm figures in a 10 mm circle,
ISO 6433) per row of the bill of materials, with a leader to its part.

- **Placement:** a column left or right of the view, or a row above or below it (a long, low view such as a fuselage takes a row), on
  one to three staggered rows when there are many; balloons stay in the frame, away from the other views, the title block and the
  dimensions, and leaders hardly ever cross.
- **Straight Leaders:** each balloon in line with its part (vertical leader above or below the view, horizontal beside it). When parts
  are too close, an info line says so and the balloons are placed freely.
- **Leaders** start from the centre of the part on the front, side and top views, and from a visible point of it on the isometric view.

**Only some parts:** balloons follow the bill of materials. To balloon only some collections, add them to
**Collections counted** (Bill of Materials panel); the list empty means everything is counted.

**One balloon for several different parts?** With **Count by: Name**, objects with the same base name (`Frame`, `Frame.001`…)
are counted as one part. Give each part its own name, or count by collection or by a custom property.
